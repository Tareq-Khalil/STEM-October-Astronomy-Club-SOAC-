OUR PARTNERS

Partners Who Propel Us Forward

We are proud to collaborate with leading educational organizations that support our mission of advancing astronomical education and research.

Art of Problem Solving

AoPS offers advanced math, science, and language arts courses, focusing on problem-solving and active learning to prepare students for top-tier colleges and competitive careers in STEM fields.

Wolfram

Wolfram Research is one of the world's most respected computer, web, and cloud software companies, providing powerful computational tools for astronomical research and education.

Voyager Factory

Voyager Factory is a leading Egypt-based manufacturer of telescopes and astronomical accessories, dedicated to advancing observational astronomy by providing high-quality, locally crafted instruments.
